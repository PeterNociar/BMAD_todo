Todo app: colour themes, round 1 (Ledger layout)

Four palettes for the chosen Ledger layout with the round check from C, each in light and dark. The neutrals change register; the interactive accent (focus ring, caret, check fill) stays near-ink in every variation, so the age bar is the only strong colour on screen.

Age gradient, same method everywhere: under 1h is the fresh green, 24h or more is the overdue red. Between 1h and 24h, t = (hours − 1) / 23, interpolated in OKLCH (lightness and chroma linear, hue sweeping green → amber → orange → red). Every stop is at least 3:1 against the row surface. Completed text (text-muted) is at least 4.5:1 against both background and surface. All hex values and contrast ratios are listed in the <style> comments.

1. Slate

Cool and composed. Blue-grey neutrals, the closest to the original Ledger direction.

Accent = muted slate-blue (desaturated, near the text hue) for focus ring, caret and check fill.
